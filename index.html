Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does treatment cost? +

Treatment costs vary depending on individual patient needs. Following your initial consultation and 3D scan, we provide a complete, itemized cost breakdown before any work begins.

How long does Invisalign treatment take? +

Most adult cases take between 6 to 18 months, depending on alignment goals. You'll begin seeing visible improvements within the first 8 weeks.

Are dental implants painful? +

No! Implants are placed using local anesthesia and gentle precision techniques. Most patients report feeling minimal discomfort, often manageable with basic over-the-counter medication.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes, we partner with CareCredit and offer customized internal monthly installment plans with 0% APR options for qualified patients.
